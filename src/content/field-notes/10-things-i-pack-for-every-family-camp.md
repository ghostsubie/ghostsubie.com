After dozens of family weekends in the Ghost, our packing list has been edited by reality. Here's what survived — and what got cut.

## The 10 that always go

1. **Water plus a way to make more.** A few gallons in the rig and a filter in the go-bag. Running out of water ends trips; everything else is negotiable.
2. **First-aid kit we actually know how to use.** Stocked for the realistic stuff: blisters, burns, splinters, allergic reactions. Practice with it at home first.
3. **Tire repair and a compressor.** A plug kit and a 12V compressor have saved more trips than any other single item. Flat tires don't care about your itinerary.
4. **Fire kit.** Lighter, ferro rod, and dry tinder in a waterproof bag. Redundancy matters when the one lighter gets wet.
5. **Headlamps for everyone.** One per person, plus spare batteries. The sun sets exactly when you're mid-task, every time.
6. **Camp chairs.** Cheap ones are fine. Sitting on the ground gets old by night two, and the campfire is the whole point.
7. **Cooler and one real meal plan.** Not a gourmet spread — just knowing what dinner is before we leave. Hungry kids at a dark campsite is a situation, not an adventure.
8. **Weather layers.** Texas will swing 40 degrees on you without apology. A warm layer per person lives in the rig year-round.
9. **Paper map and offline maps.** Phones die, signal lies. A paper map of the area and downloaded offline maps are cheap insurance.
10. **Trash bags and the leave-no-trace habit.** We pack out everything, including the stuff the last campers left. The next family gets the campsite we wish we'd found.

## The 3 I stopped packing

1. **The giant camp table.** Used it twice in two years. The tailgate and a camp chair work fine, and that's one less bulky thing to wrestle.
2. **Duplicate tools.** I carried two of everything "just in case" until I realized one good set, maintained, beats two mediocre ones gathering rust.
3. **"Just in case" bulk food.** Three extra days of food for a two-night trip. We ate the same rotation anyway. Now: the meal plan, plus one backup dinner. Done.

The list gets shorter every year, and the trips get better. Funny how that works.
